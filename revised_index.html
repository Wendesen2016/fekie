About me

I am a sociologist, lecturer, and researcher at Mekdela Amba University in Ethiopia, where I have taught since 2019. I hold an MA in Sociology from Bahir Dar University and a BA in Sociology from Debre Berhan University. My academic interests include social inequality, rural livelihoods, conflict and displacement, migration, and educational equity. My work brings together university teaching and social research, with a focus on how social conditions shape people’s opportunities, how communities respond to disruption, and how research can deepen understanding of social challenges and the lived experiences of communities in Ethiopia.

Social inequality and livelihoods

Rural livelihoods, poverty, social inequality, and differences in opportunity within and between communities.

Conflict, migration and displacement

The social effects of conflict, movement, displacement, and the ways households and communities adapt to disruption.

Educational equity

Unequal access to learning, rural education, and the social implications of gaps in educational resources and technology.

Community resilience

How communities respond to difficult circumstances and sustain their livelihoods amid social and economic change.
